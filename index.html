FaithTales

Privacy Policy

App: FaithTales for Android
Developer: Justin Sudhakar

Effective date: 12 October 2026

In short: FaithTales does not ask for your name, your email address, or any account. From our review of the app, we did not find any feature that collects personal information or sends it to the developer or to anyone else. The app has no advertisements. The favorites and display choices you make stay on your device.

1. Introduction and scope

FaithTales is a free, illustrated Bible story app for children and families, with 365 stories. This policy explains, in plain language, what the FaithTales Android app does and does not do with information. It applies only to the FaithTales app.

The app is published by Justin Sudhakar, an individual developer. In this policy, "I" and "me" mean Justin Sudhakar.

2. Information the app collects

FaithTales does not need an account or a sign-in. It has no forms, no comments, no chat, and no way to upload your own content.

From a review of the app's code, FaithTales does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, or advertising or device identifiers. It is not designed to send information about you to me or to any other party, and it is not designed to ask for Android permissions such as location, camera, or contacts.

3. Favorites and display settings saved on your device

If you mark stories as favorites, switch between Day and Night mode, or choose how the illustrations are shown, the app remembers those choices on your device so they are there the next time you open it. This information stays on your device and is not sent to me.

Anything you type into the search box is used only while you are searching. The app does not save it.

4. Offline content and illustrations

All 365 stories, their illustrations, and the fonts used in the app are stored inside the app. You can read the stories without an internet connection.

5. Sharing

When you tap Share, your phone's own share menu opens, or the text is copied so you can paste it yourself. The text is only the story title in a short sentence, for example: Read "Story title" on FaithTales. What happens next depends on the app and the person you choose. The app itself does not send it anywhere, and the other app's own privacy policy applies to it.

6. Internet access and third-party services

The app is not designed to connect to the internet or to any server of mine. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no crash-reporting services, and it does not load fonts or other content from other websites.

The app may offer a link to this privacy policy. If you open it, the policy opens in your phone's web browser, after a short question meant for adults. That visit is handled by your browser and your internet connection, not by FaithTales.

Google Play, Android, and your phone's maker are run by other companies. They have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover.

7. Children's privacy

FaithTales is made for children and families to enjoy together. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no advertising, no accounts, no chat, and no content that children can post. Parents and guardians who have questions are welcome to contact me using the details below.

8. Security

FaithTales is not designed to collect personal information or to send data off your device, so there is no personal information about you for me to store or protect. The favorites and settings the app saves are kept in the app's own storage area on your phone. This policy does not make any promises about the security features of your phone, of Android, or of services run by other companies.

9. Keeping and deleting your information

I do not hold any information about you. The favorites and settings saved on your device remain there until you remove them. You can remove them at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings (on many phones this is under Settings, Apps, FaithTales, Storage) or by uninstalling the app. The exact menus differ from phone to phone.

10. Android backups

Depending on your device and backup settings, Android may include some app data in a device backup. The exact information included depends on your device and backup configuration. I cannot access or manage your device backups. Please refer to your device and backup provider's settings and privacy information for details.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and its effective date. The latest version is always available at this web address.

12. Contact

If you have a question about this policy or about privacy in FaithTales, please write to kaangeyan@gmail.com.
